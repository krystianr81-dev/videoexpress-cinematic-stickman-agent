## Visual grammar

The reference presents a vertical illustrated western world: oversized warm cream circular heads; narrow black stick arms and legs; colored costume torsos; heavy, slightly imperfect ink outlines; and expressive drawn faces. Detailed painted western environments supply texture and depth around simpler characters. Cel shading and layered scenery contribute dimension while retaining a 2D appearance.

Bold white lettering with a strong black outline appears near the lower center. The observed caption treatment presents short groups of one or two words. The adaptation targets that treatment through editor captions, rather than generating text inside artwork.

## Narration and story construction

The transcript clearly supports a voice-over narrative: a cowboy story about Slow Eddie drawing second, followed by a reflex explanation. The adaptation uses the general sequence of hook, escalating action, explanatory reveal, and thematic payoff, while replacing the premise, protagonist, plot, and spoken text completely.

Brisk narration at roughly 185 words per minute is a pacing estimate from transcript amount and reference duration. It is not an audio-derived measurement. The sample times do not establish every edit or shot duration; the new eight-scene plan is an independent pacing design rather than an exact cut map.

## Lip-sync interpretation

Continuous narrator-to-character lip-sync is not required to explain the observed voice-over storytelling. Exact syllable-to-mouth synchronization in the original has not been verified. Accordingly, the new workflow deliberately chooses an offscreen narrator with lip-sync disabled. That is a production decision, not proof that the reference contains no synchronized mouths.

## Adaptation and completion status

It preserves the broad illustrated treatment and narrative rhythm, without copying the cowboy plot or audio. Nora's master and the generated opening establish the current production identity; subsequent prompts preserve that identity and the observed set direction.
